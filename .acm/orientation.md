### Active operational rules

- Before a first-contact Destination hunch, read relevant current conversation, discover applicable higher-scope Destination, and inspect available target-local signal proportionally. Do not treat the latest request as the whole purpose when earlier context carries it.
- Preserve three separate facts in destination work: the desired real-world outcome, the responsible evidence-led route, and mechanisms intentionally left open. An unknown mechanism never erases a confirmed outcome.
- Destination hunches are sourced questions, not operating authority. The operator settles Destination; append corrections rather than rewriting history.
- Keep the compact interview inexpensive: one discriminating question is the default; deeper sourced questioning is an escalation for thin, high-stakes cases, not a standing ceremony.
- Every Trail entry declares `Model: <identity>` or `Model: unavailable`. Repeated same-model bounded silence is a plateau to name and escalate, not cross-model convergence.
- Orientation is mandatory when a confirmed Destination changes. Open the Trail prediction first; then replace this current block from an open target pass, not inherited scores.
- Use rubric scores as disposable plateau telemetry. They describe current evidence and never prescribe the next route.
- Preserve the enclosing mission across instrumental targets through both evaluation and probe Trails; return when the registered evidence is obtained or additional cost is disproportionate.
- Keep Improve-grade route comparison, structural challenge, and human-legibility checks for every decision-bearing run while scaling target evidence and durable narration to risk.
